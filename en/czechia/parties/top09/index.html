Reading codes

Red: alarming. Green: reassuring. Amber: half-way. Grey: neutral or consistent.

Promise statuses

  • Delivered done, with a measured effect for people
  • Half-way partly done, diluted, or with mixed results
  • Consistent in opposition, the party voted for its promise but was outvoted
  • Contradiction the party acted against what it claims, established by at least three sources
  • Rights alert an independent authority found the measure breached fundamental rights

⚠ Democratic watch point (in red, on party cards): a programme promise that would restrict a democratic guarantee (free elections, independent courts, freedoms, equality before the law, checks and balances, personal rights) and that an independent authority has found contrary to fundamental rights.   • Broken No tax rises (SPOLU, 2021): corporate tax raised to 21% from 2024 source
  • Broken More for research: real R&D spending down about 17% from 2021 to 2024 (Demagog) source
  • Kept, made a difference Consolidate public finances: deficit down from 3.7% to 2.0% of GDP between 2023 and 2024 (ČSÚ) source · measured effect

3 to 4 concrete promises from its latest programme, each checked against an official act or an independent tracker. “Made a difference” needs an independent measure of the effect on people; a promise adopted without measurable effect, symbolic or quickly reversed counts as “on paper” (a quarter of a point). “Partly” also covers a promise the party pushed but others blocked. A promise that needed power the party never held cannot be tested.

If you're considering voting for TOP 09, it's worth looking beyond the slogans.

It is the party of science. While it sat in government, with its own minister for science until 2023, real research spending fell by about 17% (Demagog).

It is also the party of fiscal prudence. After a 2021 campaign promising no tax rises, its government raised corporate tax from 19% to 21% to slow the growth of debt.

In October 2026, in opposition, it voted to reject the coalition's bill abolishing licence fees. The motion failed, with 81 votes of 177.

To be fair, its coalition's licence-fee rise helped lift Czechia seven places to 10th in press freedom (RSF).
