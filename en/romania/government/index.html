Who governs in Romania?

Who's at the wheel

Luca Niculescu (independent, former state secretary at the Foreign Ministry), Prime Minister since 20 October 2026. PSD holds 11 ministries plus a delegate minister, and the rest, including Finance, went to independents. PNL, UDMR, the national minorities and the 'Uniți pentru România' group support it from outside.

On 5 May 2026 a PSD-AUR censure motion brought down Ilie Bolojan's government with 281 votes. Three attempts then failed: Eugen Tomac withdrew, and Siegfried Mureșan (182 votes) and Adrian Veștea (189) fell short of the 233 needed. On 5 October President Dan designated Niculescu, who won 280 votes on 20 October. PNL has pledged not to file or back a censure motion until 1 March 2027.

What the government promised

  • Niculescu government programme 2026-2028 (October 2026): no tax rises in 2027, pension indexation, 2027 budget by 1 December, deficit below 3% of GDP by 2030
  • Summary of the programme's priorities (Puterea, October 2026)

What independent promise trackers found

  • No independent promise tracker found for either government. Euronews Romania's review of the Bolojan programme after nine months (6 April 2026) found clear results only on taxes. The planned 20% cut in public jobs was 'far from done' (about 25,000 posts, under 2%), a quarter of 226 agencies were still awaiting mergers, and the ban on drawing a pension and a salary at once 'stayed on paper'. source

The results, as measured by independent bodies

Findings from independent public research bodies. No label, no verdict: the figures speak.

  • Eurostat: public deficit 7.9% of GDP in 2025, down from 9.3% in 2024 but still the highest in the EU; public debt 59.3% of GDP, up from 54.8%. source
  • INS: annual inflation 10.42% in June 2026 (services +13.67%), down to 6.6% in September 2026, still the highest in the EU. source
  • INS: GDP flat in Q2 2026 on the previous quarter and down 1.9% year on year (seasonally adjusted), so the economy is in technical recession. Household consumption fell 3.1%, and fixed investment rose 19.9%. source
