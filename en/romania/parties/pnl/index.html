Reading codes

Red: alarming. Green: reassuring. Amber: half-way. Grey: neutral or consistent.

Promise statuses

  • Delivered done, with a measured effect for people
  • Half-way partly done, diluted, or with mixed results
  • Consistent in opposition, the party voted for its promise but was outvoted
  • Contradiction the party acted against what it claims, established by at least three sources
  • Rights alert an independent authority found the measure breached fundamental rights

⚠ Democratic watch point (in red, on party cards): a programme promise that would restrict a democratic guarantee (free elections, independent courts, freedoms, equality before the law, checks and balances, personal rights) and that an independent authority has found contrary to fundamental rights. On the posterNo public pension on top of a public salary
⚠ Rights alertPNL started the bill making public employees choose within 30 days between their pension and their salary, with exceptions for elected officials, teachers, doctors and others; the Chamber passed it 215 to 3 in June 2023, under the PSD-PNL coalition. On 5 October 2023 the Constitutional Court, on a High Court referral, unanimously found that the ban breached the fundamental right to work, and that suspending pensions breached the right to property. PNL leader Nicolae Ciucă did not contest it and said a solution 'can be discussed'. Bolojan's 2025 programme planned a new ban, which 'stayed on paper'. source · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5 · 6
Also worth knowing
  • Bolojan's government planned to cut 20% of public-sector jobs; after nine months about 25,000 posts had gone, under 2%, and a quarter of 226 agencies were still awaiting mergers. source
  • Its programme banned drawing a public pension and a public salary at the same time; Euronews found in April 2026 that the ban had 'stayed on paper'. source
  • After PSD brought down its government, PNL went into opposition but voted for the PSD-dominated Niculescu cabinet and signed a pledge not to back a censure motion until 1 March 2027. Bolojan said it was 'not a political deal with PSD'. source
To be fair
  • Its government cut the public deficit from 9.3% of GDP in 2024 to 7.9% in 2025 (Eurostat).
  • Its government passed the magistrates' pension reform: retirement age rising in steps to 65, net pension capped at 70% of the last net income (cleared by the Constitutional Court in February 2026).
  • Its government raised the royalties paid by OMV Petrom by 40% (December 2025).

  • Kept on paper Ban drawing a public pension and a salary at the same time: 'stayed on paper' source

3 to 4 concrete promises from its latest programme, each checked against an official act or an independent tracker. “Made a difference” needs an independent measure of the effect on people; a promise adopted without measurable effect, symbolic or quickly reversed counts as “on paper” (a quarter of a point). “Partly” also covers a promise the party pushed but others blocked. A promise that needed power the party never held cannot be tested.

If you're considering voting for PNL, it's worth looking beyond the slogans.

In 2024 its nominee Marcel Boloș ran the Finance Ministry while the deficit reached 9.3% of GDP. He says he warned the Prime Minister every month from September.

Its own government then promised to slim the state by a fifth. Nine months later, under 2% of public jobs had gone, and its ban on drawing a public pension and a salary at once had stayed on paper; an earlier PNL version had been struck down in 2023 for breaching the right to work.

In October 2026 it voted for a PSD-dominated cabinet, specifying that this was 'not a political deal with PSD'.

To be fair, its government brought the deficit down from 9.3% to 7.9% of GDP in one year (Eurostat).
