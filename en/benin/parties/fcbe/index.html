Benin

Presidential republic · about 14 million people · a parliament with no opposition since February 2026

Before you read
  • The approach: we set what parties say against what they do, and every fact links to an official or independent source.
  • Neutrality: the same rules apply to every party, left or right, in government or in opposition. Barotico recommends no vote.
  • A summary, not the whole story: read the programmes, follow several media, and make up your own mind.
  • Dates: the date at the top is the profile's last edit. Each section gives its own source and, where it applies, the date of the data or of the last check; in “Watch list”, some dates are upcoming deadlines.
Next vote≈ 74 monthsNext general elections expected in 2033 (legislative and local in January, presidential in April; no date set)
The November 2025 constitutional revision gave the president, deputies, mayors and local councillors seven-year terms. The deputies and councillors elected on 11 January 2026 and the president elected on 12 April 2026 are therefore due for re-election in 2033 (month estimated from the 2026 calendar). Deputies voted in July 2026 to suspend the electoral commission (CENA) 'provisionally', citing the absence of major elections for six years; the body that will run the 2033 vote has not yet been defined. source

Reading codes

Red: alarming. Green: reassuring. Amber: half-way. Grey: neutral or consistent.

Promise statuses

  • Delivered done, with a measured effect for people
  • Half-way partly done, diluted, or with mixed results
  • Consistent in opposition, the party voted for its promise but was outvoted
  • Contradiction the party acted against what it claims, established by at least three sources
  • Rights alert an independent authority found the measure breached fundamental rights

⚠ Democratic watch point (in red, on party cards): a programme promise that would restrict a democratic guarantee (free elections, independent courts, freedoms, equality before the law, checks and balances, personal rights) and that an independent authority has found contrary to fundamental rights. Political goals are never flagged, only the mechanism.

The same rules apply to every party, left or right, in government or in opposition. Barotico does not recommend any vote.

On the posterAn independent opposition party, 'not subordinate' to others: 'We are opposed, we are not the same' (Paul Hounkpè, March 2025 and 9 April 2026)
ContradictionIn August 2025 one of its leaders, Idrissou Bako, said the party had 'decided to be in the pro-presidential camp in 2026'. Its national council joined Wadagni's majority on 9 May 2026, a month after the vote; its administrative secretary cited 'the agreements we signed with the two parties of the majority'. In November 2026 it was discussing a merger with the UP-R or the BR. In March 2025 a Nouvelle Tribune analysis already called its sponsorship talks with the majority a contradiction with its stated opposition. source · 2 · 3 · 4 · 5
On the posterUrgent national reconciliation, through dialogue
Half-wayIn October 2019, while the rest of the opposition refused, its Hounkpè wing took part in the political dialogue called by Talon. The dialogue led to the amnesty of 31 October 2019, which freed people detained after the April 2019 election. The constitutional revision voted the next day had not been discussed there, and the FCBE withdrew from the process the day before the vote. source · 2 · 3
Also worth knowing
  • On 9 April 2026 its candidate told voters he and Wadagni were 'not the same' and 'opposed'; on 9 May its national council decided to join Wadagni's presidential majority from inauguration day. source
  • In March 2025 it said it remained independent and did not plan to back another party, while negotiating with the presidential majority for the 28 sponsorships it needed; in September 2025 one of its mayors said the FCBE intended to leave the opposition after the 2026 election. source
  • During the campaign its candidate pointed to the absence of any opposition in Parliament and local councils; a month later the party joined the majority that holds all 109 seats. source
To be fair
  • Put up the only alternative ticket in the 2026 presidential election (5.73%)
  • Its candidate raised public debt and the detention of political prisoners during the campaign
  • In 2021 its leader proposed that President Talon release Reckya Madougou and Joël Aïvo
