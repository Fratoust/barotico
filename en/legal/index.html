Barotico

Legal and privacy

Barotico names people who hold or seek public office and reports official acts concerning them. These rules exist to respect their rights, the presumption of innocence and your privacy.

Legal notice

Publisher and editor-in-chief: François Toussaint, private individual. Non-commercial site, funded only by readers' voluntary contributions. Contact: contact@barotico.com.

Hosting: OVH SAS, 2 rue Kellermann, 59100 Roubaix, France (European Union). Information required by Spanish Law 34/2002 on information society services (LSSI), article 10.

Fonts: Young Serif, Public Sans and IBM Plex Mono, under the SIL Open Font License, hosted with the site.

Editorial charter: court proceedings

Purpose. Inform the public about the integrity and conduct of people in public life, in connection with their public role. This is journalistic work protected by freedom of information (article 20 of the Spanish Constitution, article 11 of the EU Charter of Fundamental Rights, article 85 GDPR). It is free to read, edited, and every fact is checked against a source, the conditions set by the EU Court of Justice in July 2026 (case C-199/24).

Who. Only parties, their national leaders, ministers of the last ten years, and current national and European members of parliament. Never relatives, staff or private individuals.

What. Only official acts: court decisions, formal charges, referrals to trial, parliamentary sanctions, regulator decisions, party expulsions. Each with its exact status and a link to a recognised source. Complaints, allegations and rumours are never listed.

Presumption of innocence. Unless a conviction is final, the person is presumed innocent, and the page says so next to the case.

Updates. Statuses are checked on every date in the watch list and at least once a month in election periods; the date of the last check is shown. An acquittal or dismissal is shown as visibly as the earlier stage, then removed after twelve months or earlier on request.

Time limit. Ten years. A conviction erased from criminal records (rehabilitation, cancelación de antecedentes) is removed.

No database. No search by name, no individual profiles, no photos of people, no paywall.

Satire. The one-line summaries are opinions based on facts sourced on the same card.

Corrections, right of reply and rectification

Anyone mentioned, and any reader, can report an inaccurate fact or an outdated status at contact@barotico.com, with the source.

A rectification about a fact concerning you is published in full within three days, next to the information and without comment, as Spanish Organic Law 2/1984 requires of the media. Parties' documented replies are added to their card.

Privacy policy

Controller: the publisher named above. Contact for any data protection request: contact@barotico.com.

Visitors. No account, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers. Your language choice is kept in your own browser (local storage) only to remember it; you can clear it at any time. The values mirror is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent. Fonts are hosted with the site, so displaying it sends no data to third parties. The host keeps technical logs, including IP addresses, for security: OVHcloud, in the European Union, under its own retention terms.

Donations go through Buy Me a Coffee, which processes payment data under its own privacy policy; we only receive what you choose to share there.

People mentioned. Data: name, public role, party, public statements and votes, official acts listed in the court proceedings section. Sources: official bodies and recognised media. Purpose and legal basis: informing the public on matters of public interest (legitimate interest, article 6(1)(f) GDPR, weighed against freedom of information; article 85 GDPR for the journalistic part). Retention: as set out in the editorial charter.

Your rights: access, rectification, erasure, objection and restriction, by writing to contact@barotico.com; answer within one month. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, aepd.es).

Artificial intelligence and editorial responsibility

Research and drafting are assisted by AI tools. Every profile is reviewed by a person and published under the editorial responsibility of François Toussaint, in line with article 50 of the EU AI Act. We say so openly.

Barotico is cooperative and independent

No ads, and no money from political parties. If Barotico helps you, buy us a coffee: it pays for updates, fact-checking and new countries.
