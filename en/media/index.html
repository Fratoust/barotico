Media guide

Where to get your news

We never label a media outlet “biased” ourselves. We show who owns it, what regulators and parliaments have decided, and how much trust it earns, measured by independent surveys. The same rules apply to public and private, left- and right-leaning media.

How to spot editorialised news

News rests on verified, sourced facts and gives several points of view. Commentary interprets facts from one point of view. Opinion is a personal judgement that may do without reliable sources.

Ask five questions: who is speaking, what are they saying, what is their argument, in what tone, and with what intention? Be wary of content that plays on emotion.

Check who owns the outlet, and whether a fact-checker holds a valid EFCSN or IFCN certification.
