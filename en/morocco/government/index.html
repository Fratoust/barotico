Reading codes

Red: alarming. Green: reassuring. Amber: half-way. Grey: neutral or consistent.

Promise statuses

  • Delivered done, with a measured effect for people
  • Half-way partly done, diluted, or with mixed results
  • Consistent in opposition, the party voted for its promise but was outvoted
  • Contradiction the party acted against what it claims, established by at least three sources
  • Rights alert an independent authority found the measure breached fundamental rights

⚠ Democratic watch point (in red, on party cards): a programme promise that would restrict a democratic guarantee (free elections, independent courts, freedoms, equality before the law, checks and balances, personal rights) and that an independent authority has found contrary to fundamental rights. Political goals are never flagged, only the mechanism.

Who governs in Morocco?

Who's at the wheel

Head of government Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri (PAM), the first woman in the post, appointed by the King on 29 September 2026. Her 32-member government, appointed on 16 October, rests on the PAM, Istiqlal, USFP and PPS (207 of 395 seats). Fouzi Lekjaa (PAM) holds Economy and Finance, Abdellatif Ouahbi stays at Justice, Nabil Benabdallah (PPS) takes Education.

The PAM came first on 23 September 2026 with 97 seats. Under Article 47 the King appointed its coordinator; the four-party alliance was announced on 12 October, and on 22 October the House of Representatives approved the 2026-2031 programme by 204 votes to 127, the RNI voting against.

In government: 207 absolute majority: 198

The parties in government hold 207 of 395 seats; an absolute majority is 198. They have 9 seats more than the majority. With the parties that support it (17 seats), the total is 224.

Seats from the 23 September 2026 election. The four-party alliance (PAM, Istiqlal, USFP, PPS) was announced on 12 October; on 22 October the House approved its programme by 204 votes to 127.

What the government promised

  • Government programme 2026-2031 (adopted 22 Oct. 2026): direct social aid raised from 500 to 1,000 DH, minimum pension of 3,000 DH, 100,000 first work experiences a year, deficit capped at 3% of GDP, 5% growth
  • Outgoing government programme 2021-2026 (Akhannouch): at least one million net jobs, women's activity rate above 30%, generalised social protection

What independent promise trackers found

  • No independent promise tracker found. Le Matin (Oct. 2026) compared the four coalition parties' campaign pledges with the government programme: the million jobs, the 7% unemployment target and the wealth taxes were dropped. source
  • TelQuel (20 Oct. 2026): the health chapter of the new programme contains neither figures nor deadlines. source
  • Not independent: the outgoing government's own 'Voie des réalisations' review of four years in office (2025). source

The results, as measured by independent bodies

Findings from independent public research bodies. No label, no verdict: the figures speak.

  • HCP: unemployment 12.3% in 2021 and 13.0% in 2025 (1.621 million unemployed); 37.2% for 15-24 year-olds; 193,000 net jobs created in 2025 after 82,000 in 2024. source
  • HCP: consumer prices rose 6.6% in 2022, then 0.8% in 2025. source
  • Cour des comptes: health-insurance (AMO) coverage rose from 60% to 70% between 2022 and 2024 (25.62 million people with open rights), but more than 11 million remain outside, and several schemes are in deficit. source
