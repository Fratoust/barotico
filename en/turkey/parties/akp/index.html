Turkey (Türkiye)

Presidential republic since 2018 · about 86 million people

Before you read
  • The approach: we set what parties say against what they do, and every fact links to an official or independent source.
  • Neutrality: the same rules apply to every party, left or right, in government or in opposition. Barotico recommends no vote.
  • A summary, not the whole story: read the programmes, follow several media, and make up your own mind.
  • Dates: the date at the top is the profile's last edit. Each section gives its own source and, where it applies, the date of the data or of the last check.
Next vote538 daysNext national vote: presidential and parliamentary elections, regular date 7 May 2028 (could be brought forward)
The President and the 600 deputies are elected on the same day for five years; the last vote was on 14 May 2023, with a presidential run-off on 28 May. President Erdoğan's chief adviser Mehmet Uçum says the regular date is 7 May 2028 and has floated 16 April 2028 instead: if at least 360 deputies vote to renew the elections early, Erdoğan, who has already served two terms, could stand again without a constitutional amendment. According to Uçum, parliament could take that decision between 9 and 15 February 2028. The Supreme Election Council (YSK) has not yet published a calendar. source

Reading codes

Red: alarming. Green: reassuring. Amber: half-way. Grey: neutral or consistent.

Promise statuses

  • Delivered done, with a measured effect for people
  • Half-way partly done, diluted, or with mixed results
  • Consistent in opposition, the party voted for its promise but was outvoted
  • Contradiction the party acted against what it claims, established by at least three sources
  • Rights alert an independent authority found the measure breached fundamental rights

⚠ Democratic watch point (in red, on party cards): a programme promise that would restrict a democratic guarantee (free elections, independent courts, freedoms, equality before the law, checks and balances, personal rights) and that an independent authority has found contrary to fundamental rights. Political goals are never flagged, only the mechanism.

The same rules apply to every party, left or right, in government or in opposition. Barotico does not recommend any vote.

On the posterSingle-digit inflation (2023 manifesto)
Half-wayAnnual inflation fell from 75.45% in May 2024 to 29.73% in September 2026 (TÜİK); the independent ENAG group of economists measured 120.66% and 46.61% for the same months. In September 2026 its own Medium-Term Programme raised the 2026 year-end forecast from 16% to 28.4%, about 7 points of it due to the war in the Middle East according to the Central Bank, and moved single digits to 2029. source · 2 · 3
On the posterRebuild the earthquake zone: 319,000 homes within a year, 650,000 in all (2023)
Half-wayThe overall target was later cut to 453,000. The government says 455,357 homes and workplaces were completed by 27 December 2025, nearly three years after the February 2023 quakes; President Erdoğan said it had kept its word. In its 2026 budget proposal, the disaster agency AFAD expected title deeds to be handed over for only 10% of the homes built on Treasury land by the end of 2025. source · 2 · 3
On the posterMore freedoms: a 'libertarian' new constitution (2023 manifesto)
⚠ Rights alertOn 29 April 2017 the telecoms authority under its government blocked all of Wikipedia under the Internet Law passed by its majority. On 26 December 2019 the Constitutional Court ruled 10 to 6 that the block breached freedom of expression, and access was restored. On 11 October 2023 the Court annulled that law's procedure for blocking online content over 'personality rights' (Article 9) as an interference with freedom of expression and of the press without safeguards; no replacement was passed, and the annulment took effect in October 2024. source · 2 · 3
On the posterEnd emergency rule and keep the country safe (2018)
⚠ Rights alertWhen emergency rule ended in July 2018, its majority, with the MHP, passed Law 7145 keeping emergency-era powers for three years, extended in 2021. On 30 June 2022 the Constitutional Court annulled a series of provisions on substance, including the ban on meetings after midnight (freedom of assembly), detention reviews on file without hearing the detainee (personal liberty), passport cancellations extended to spouses (freedom of travel), rank removals without individual assessment (private life), a bar on compensation claims (access to court) and open-ended transfers of suspects' family data (data protection). It upheld the governors' power to restrict movement and gatherings. We found no reaction from the party. source · 2 · 3 · 4
Also worth knowing

3 to 4 concrete promises from its latest programme, each checked against an official act or an independent tracker. “Made a difference” needs an independent measure of the effect on people; a promise adopted without measurable effect, symbolic or quickly reversed counts as “on paper” (a quarter of a point). “Partly” also covers a promise the party pushed but others blocked. A promise that needed power the party never held cannot be tested.

If you're considering voting for AKP, it's worth looking beyond the slogans.

It promised single-digit inflation in 2023. In September 2026 TÜİK measured 29.73%, the independent ENAG group 46.61%. Its own programme now aims for single digits in 2029.

It pledged 319,000 earthquake homes within a year and 650,000 in all. The target was then lowered to 453,000; by December 2025, the government says, 455,357 homes and workplaces were finished.

Its manifesto promised a 'pluralist, libertarian' constitution. In 2026 Freedom House scores judicial independence in Turkey at 1 out of 4.

To be fair, TÜİK measured unemployment at 7.8% in August 2026, close to its 7% pledge.

No official programme found online yet.
