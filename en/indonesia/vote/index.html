Voting in Indonesia

Before the next vote

How to vote, in practice

The KPU draws up the permanent voters' list (DPT): 204.8 million voters for 2024, including 1.75 million abroad. Voters cast their ballot at their assigned polling station; check your entry with the KPU before the vote. Average turnout fell to 68% in the November 2024 local elections.

Last turnout: 81.14% in the February 2024 DPR election (KPU) source

The electoral system

  • President and vice-president run as a pair; in February 2024 Prabowo Subianto and Gibran Rakabuming Raka won in the first round with 58.58% (96.2 million votes).
  • The 580 DPR seats are shared among party lists by proportional representation, but only parties with at least 4% of national valid votes get seats (Art. 414, Law 7/2017).
  • In 2024 everything was voted on the same day with five ballots; after the 2025 Constitutional Court ruling, national and local elections will be held separately.

Sources: DPR research centre: 2024 presidential and legislative results · detik: the end of the five-ballot election

Blank vote: what it says, what it changes

In Indonesia, national ballots have no blank-vote box, and only valid votes count towards seats and thresholds. The one place where voters can pick 'nobody' is a local election with a single candidate: the empty box (kotak kosong) can win, and the vote is then held again.

What it is

  • Whether a ballot is valid or invalid is decided by the election commission (KPU), which records invalid ballots as a separate figure. source
  • In a single-candidate local election, the ballot shows the candidate next to an empty box; when the empty box wins, the KPU organises a new election. source

How it is counted

  • The 4% threshold for DPR seats is calculated on national valid votes only (Art. 414, Law 7/2017): invalid ballots are left out of the base. source
  • Voting is presented as a right that citizens choose to use: the KPU measures participation as the share of voters who use their right to vote. source

The figures

  • 81.14%turnout in the February 2024 DPR election (KPU). source
  • 68%average turnout in the November 2024 local elections, lower than in the general election (KPU). source

What it changes for the result

  • Votes for parties under 4% also elect nobody: in 2024 the 5.88 million PPP votes (3.87%) and 4.26 million PSI votes (2.80%) produced no seat. source

Questions to ask yourself

  • If no option represents me, do I want that refusal to be counted? Check above how it is counted here.
  • Do I reject every option, or is one of them less far from my ideas than the others?
  • Do I want to weigh on the result, or to make a refusal count?
  • Is the race close, is the country going through a crisis, could the election tip one way or the other? The closer it is, the more the outcome rests on the votes given to candidates.
  • Blank, spoilt or abstention: what does the law of this country do with each one?

Elsewhere, a blank vote has effects

  • FranceCounted separately and published since 2014, but outside the votes cast: no effect on the result or on thresholds. source
  • SpainBlank votes count as valid votes. They raise the number of votes a list needs to pass the 3% bar for seats. Only an empty envelope is blank: a blank sheet inside is spoilt. source source
  • ColombiaIf blank votes win a majority of valid votes (first presidential round, governors, mayors, assemblies), the election is held again once, without the same candidates. source
  • PeruIf spoilt and blank votes, together or separately, exceed two thirds of votes cast, the election is annulled. source
  • ItalyBlank ballots do not count as valid votes, but in abrogative referendums they count towards the turnout quorum: voting blank helps reach it, abstaining helps defeat it. source
  • Nevada (United States)Since 1975, ballots offer “None of these candidates” in statewide and presidential races. It can come first, but it never wins: the candidate with the most votes is elected. source
  • IndiaSince a 2013 Supreme Court ruling, voting machines offer “None of the above”. It cannot cancel an election: the candidate with the most votes still wins. source

For lawmakers and citizens who want to change the rule: each model has a cost. Rerunning an election takes time and money, a low threshold can leave seats empty, and counting blank votes changes majorities and thresholds.

Method and missing data What is missing from this page, why, and how the choices were made.

Party cards cover every party with seats in the DPR or at least 3% of the 2024 vote: the eight parliamentary parties plus PPP (3.87%, no seats). Left-right positions use V-Party's economic left-right scale only (2019 expert survey, rescaled to -2/+2), because no Chapel Hill survey covers Indonesia and V-Party has no general left-right score; this can place religious-conservative parties such as PKS left of centre.

Press sources come only from validated outlets (ANTARA, Kompas, CNN Indonesia and detik, Liputan6 and TVRI, Tempo) and official bodies. The approval figure is the latest recognised poll found through them (February 2026); later polls exist but were not reported by these outlets. No party publishes an audited income breakdown, so funding shares are left empty; no programme files could be opened, and promises come from the 2024 presidential platforms or the government programme each party backs. No independent promise tracker or rejection poll was found.

The judicial record lists only official acts (court rulings, KPK suspect status, parliamentary sanctions) within ten years, and only for people whose party membership at the time is confirmed: cases where the party disputes membership are left out, and people who have since left the party are marked as such. The homicide rate is the latest UNODC year available (2022). The 2029 election month is an estimate: no date has been set.
