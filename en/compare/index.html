How people live: official figures

Each country is compared with the average of its own region, from the same source (the EU for EU members, sub-Saharan Africa for Senegal, and so on). The colour shows whether the value is more or less favourable on that indicator; a word gives the direction (above, close, below) for colour-blind readers. No overall score is invented.

More favourable on this indicatorClose to the reference, or middle of the scaleLess favourable on this indicator

These labels compare a country with the reference shown for that indicator. They do not rate a country overall. Whether a higher value is favourable depends on the indicator: a higher life expectancy is, a higher homicide rate is not.

Continents follow the UN M49 standard, a statistical convention that implies no political affiliation. Source: UN M49
