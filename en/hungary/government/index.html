Who governs in Hungary?

Who's at the wheel

Péter Magyar (Tisza), Prime Minister since 9 May 2026, with a 16-member cabinet sworn in on 12 May. András Baka, elected by Parliament, has been President of the Republic since 19 August 2026.

Tisza won 141 of 199 seats, including 96 of the 106 districts, on a record 79.56% turnout. Parliament elected Magyar by 140 votes to 54 (one abstention). Viktor Orbán gave up his seat on 25 April and was re-elected Fidesz president in June. Gergely Gulyás led the Fidesz-KDNP group until July, when János Bóka took over.

What the government promised

  • Tisza programme (February 2026, 240 pages): join the European Public Prosecutor's Office, unlock EU funds, 9% income tax on the minimum wage, 1% wealth tax above HUF 1 billion, HUF 500 billion more a year for health, suspend public-media news (summary by 444)
  • 2027 budget and tax package (October 2026): 9% income tax on the minimum wage, wealth tax on billionaires, minimum pension raised to HUF 120,000, deficit 4.9% of GDP

What independent promise trackers found

  • Telex, first 100 days (August 2026, independent): promises 'partly' kept. Delivered: EU funds deal, EPPO accession approved, Sovereignty Protection Office abolished, asset-declaration and asset-recovery laws passed. Not delivered: the 'Road to prison' accountability programme, disclosure of secret government decisions, and a competition for a new public-media head. source

The results, as measured by independent bodies

Findings from independent public research bodies. No label, no verdict: the figures speak.

  • KSH: consumer prices up 17.6% on average in 2023, 4.4% in 2025, and 1.3% year on year in August 2026. source
  • EDP notification (April 2026): general government deficit 4.7% of GDP in 2025; public debt 74.6% of GDP, up from 73.5% in 2024. source
  • Amended 2026 budget (October 2026): the deficit target rose from 3.7% to 7.5% of GDP (HUF 7,110 billion); the 2027 plan assumes 4.9%. source
  • KSH (preliminary): about 72,000 births in 2025, 7.1% fewer than in 2024; total fertility rate 1.31, down from 1.39; natural decrease of 52,200. source
