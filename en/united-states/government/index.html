How to read this country profile

The profile has seven tabs. Start with the system to understand the rules of the game, then look at who governs and what the parties do. Every piece of information links to its source: click to check.

  1. How the country is run: institutions, elections, who passes the laws, and where things get stuck.

Reading codes

Red: alarming. Green: reassuring. Amber: half-way. Grey: neutral or consistent.

Promise statuses

  • Delivered done, with a measured effect for people
  • Half-way partly done, diluted, or with mixed results
  • Consistent in opposition, the party voted for its promise but was outvoted
  • Contradiction the party acted against what it claims, established by at least three sources
  • Rights alert an independent authority found the measure breached fundamental rights

⚠ Democratic watch point (in red, on party cards): a programme promise that would restrict a democratic guarantee (free elections, independent courts, freedoms, equality before the law, checks and balances, personal rights) and that an independent authority has found contrary to fundamental rights. Political goals are never flagged, only the mechanism.

The same rules apply to every party, left or right, in government or in opposition. Barotico does not recommend any vote.

Who governs in the United States?

Who's at the wheel

President Donald Trump (Republican) since 20 January 2025, with Vice President JD Vance. Republicans control both chambers of Congress until 3 January 2027; Mike Johnson is Speaker of the House. In the 3 November 2026 midterms, Democrats won the House and the Senate (AP race calls): from January, the President will govern with a Congress run by the opposition. Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries has declared his candidacy for Speaker; House Democrats are due to choose their nominee in mid-December. In spring 2026 the President dismissed Attorney General Pam Bondi and Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem.

Trump won the November 2024 election with 312 electoral votes to Kamala Harris's 226 and 49.8% of the popular vote to 48.3%. Republicans kept the Senate (53 seats) and the House (220 seats in 2024).

What the government promised

  • Republican platform 2024: seal the border and carry out 'the largest deportation operation in American history', end inflation, large tax cuts and no tax on tips, tariffs, no cuts to Social Security or Medicare, dominant energy production
  • One Big Beautiful Bill Act (July 2025): makes the 2017 tax cuts permanent, adds new tax breaks and cuts spending, mainly on health care for low-income people

What independent promise trackers found

  • PolitiFact MAGA-Meter (independent, Feb. 2026): of 75 promises tracked, about 19% kept, 3 compromises, 1 broken (end the Russia-Ukraine war within 24 hours), about 45% in the works and about 31% stalled, including bringing down the price of everyday goods. source
