Reading codes

Red: alarming. Green: reassuring. Amber: half-way. Grey: neutral or consistent.

Promise statuses

  • Delivered done, with a measured effect for people
  • Half-way partly done, diluted, or with mixed results
  • Consistent in opposition, the party voted for its promise but was outvoted
  • Contradiction the party acted against what it claims, established by at least three sources
  • Rights alert an independent authority found the measure breached fundamental rights

⚠ Democratic watch point (in red, on party cards): a programme promise that would restrict a democratic guarantee (free elections, independent courts, freedoms, equality before the law, checks and balances, personal rights) and that an independent authority has found contrary to fundamental rights. Political goals are never flagged, only the mechanism.

The same rules apply to every party, left or right, in government or in opposition. Barotico does not recommend any vote.

A 2025 law brought it back in a narrower form, which was upheld. source · 2 · 3 · 4
On the posterRestore authority over young offenders (Attal law, 2025)

Ground: the constitutional principle that juvenile justice must be adapted to minors and aim at their rehabilitation. A sixth fell as a rider. Attal “took note” and announced a new text. source · 2 · 3 · 4

In short

The card in a few facts, then a few ways to make up your own mind.

The barometer's verdict

If you're considering voting for Ensemble, it's worth looking beyond the slogans.

Full employment was the horizon. Unemployment, meanwhile, has climbed back to 8.3%, its highest level since 2020.

The deficit was due back under 3%. The High Council of Public Finance now pencils in “2031 at best”, for a debt that has reached 115.6% of GDP.

Leaving glyphosate was meant to take three years. Emmanuel Macron then handed the matter to Europe, where France abstained on the day the EU renewed it for ten more.

To be fair, scrapping the housing tax did leave about €200 a year with the households covered by the 2019 stage (Insee).

⚠ Democratic watch points

  • "National priority" written into the Constitution: "priority given to nationals" in jobs, public services and social housing, and the law may restrict foreigners' social benefits (constitutional bill "Citoyenneté-Identité-Immigration", 25 Jan 2024). 2024 programme: family allowances reserved for French nationals.In decision 2024-6 RIP (11 April 2024), the Conseil constitutionnel found that restricting family benefits and housing aid for lawfully resident foreigners (through long residence conditions) is a disproportionate infringement of their right to social protection. The RN proposes revising the Constitution so that this priority is allowed. source · 2 · 3 · 4Guarantee at stake: equality before the law
  • "Family reunification of foreigners may be limited or prohibited" written into the Constitution (constitutional bill, 25 Jan 2024; 2024 programme: "restriction du regroupement familial").In decision 93-325 DC (13 August 1993), the Conseil constitutionnel held that foreigners residing stably and lawfully in France have, like nationals, the right to lead a normal family life, including bringing their spouse and minor children. It struck down provisions that excluded categories of foreigners from family reunification. The RN proposes revising the Constitution to allow the restriction. source · 2 · 3Guarantee at stake: personal rights (family life, fair trial, ban on torture and collective expulsions)

The barometer's verdict · Far right

If you're considering voting for RN, it's worth looking beyond the slogans.

The RN promises to undo retirement at 64. Its repeal bill came out of committee stripped of its articles, then fell by 197 votes to 119.

Its first text ever adopted by the Assembly, on the 1968 Franco-Algerian agreement, passed by a single vote, as a resolution that binds no one.

On the retirement age, the party now speaks with two voices: since May 2026 its president says the legal age “means nothing”, while Marine Le Pen still wants a return to 62, or even 60.

To be fair, it has never governed nationally: none of its national promises has yet been put to the test.

Method and missing data What is missing from this page, why, and how the choices were made.

Party cards: France has eight cards, for the parties with a group in the National Assembly or at least 3% of the vote, ordered by seats. Smaller groups such as LIOT and the UDR appear in the seat chart without a card.

Left-right positions come from the Chapel Hill Expert Survey, rescaled from -2 to +2. "Far left" and "far right" use the same threshold on both sides (1.5) or The PopuList classification.

Democracy barometer: a party's real weight is its votes divided by all registered voters at the June 2024 European election, the last national vote in which every party ran under its own name (the 2024 legislative election was fought in alliances). Rejection comes from a single Odoxa poll (December 2025) asking the same question about every party. Pluralism and polarisation use V-Party data from 2017, when some parties had other names (LaREM, FN).

Figures: the homicide rate is our calculation from the 887 homicides recorded by Eurostat and a population of about 68 million. Party finance shares are calculated from the 2024 accounts filed with the CNCCFP; for Ensemble, public funding flows through a separate association, so its share is calculated.

Judicial records list only official acts (investigations, charges, referrals, convictions, sanctions) concerning the party, its leaders, its ministers of the last ten years and its current MPs. Complaints, press allegations and private life are left out, and acquittals or dismissals older than 12 months are removed.
