How to vote, in practice

You are registered automatically at 18 if you did the citizenship census; otherwise register online (service-public.fr) up to the sixth Friday before the vote. Vote at your polling station with an ID, or give a proxy online (maprocuration.gouv.fr). French people abroad vote at their consulate, and online for the legislative elections.

Last turnout: 66.6% in the second round of the July 2024 legislative elections (Interior Ministry) source

How people vote

  • President: direct vote, two rounds, 5-year term.
  • Deputies: one constituency each, two rounds.
  • Senators: elected by local officials (indirect vote).

How a law is passed

  1. National Assembly
  2. Senate
  3. Joint committee if they disagree
  4. Final word to the Assembly
  5. Constitutional Council if referred

Why it's complicated

The two-round system is designed to produce a clear majority. Since 2024 the Assembly has been split into three blocs with no majority: the government looks for votes bill by bill, under constant threat of a censure motion.

Why it's like thisThe Fifth Republic (1958) was built to avoid the instability of the Fourth: a strong executive and a voting system that manufactures majorities. When voters split into three blocs, the machine runs on empty.

Who's at the wheel

President: Emmanuel Macron (since 2017). Prime Minister: Sébastien Lecornu, reappointed on 10 October 2025.

Lecornu II government, a minority in the Assembly: it survived censure in October 2025 and passed the 2026 budget using Article 49.3.
