Why vote

“My vote won't change anything.” That is exactly what others need you to believe, so they can decide for you.

Whatever your choice, voting is the one moment when every voice weighs exactly as much as any other. Those who vote choose for those who stay home.

Method, sources and editorial independence

Three rules, for every party

A source for every sentence

Official vote, court ruling, published programme. If it can't be checked, it isn't shown.

The same treatment for all

The same number of promises, achievements and gaps for every party, left or right.

The exact status of every case

Investigation, trial, conviction, acquittal: the badge follows the procedure, and the presumption of innocence is restated.

A giant summary, not the whole story

Barotico condenses complex realities into a few boxes. We try to be as neutral as possible, but politics is far richer than these profiles. Read the programmes, follow several media, talk about it, and make up your own mind. If Barotico makes you want to dig deeper, it has done its job.
